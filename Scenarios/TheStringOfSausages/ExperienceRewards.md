# The String of Sausages: experience rewards

Experience points for the scenario The String of Sausages. The GM awards them at the end of the scenario (see *Awarding experience points* in `Rules/Experience.md`). Each reward is awarded at most once.

| Achievement                                                                                  | Points |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| the fugitive faced: the character confronted him in the night or found his hiding place      | 50     |
| Pansho's story heard: why he poached and why he fled                                         | 25     |
| Pansho's fate settled by the character: handed over alive or dead, let go, or freed of the iron | 100    |
| no blood shed: the character neither killed nor wounded Pansho                               | 50     |
| the road met: the character talked with at least three of the guests                         | 25     |

- A character who pretended to sleep and then left in the morning without looking for Pansho gets no points for facing him or for settling his fate.
- Letting Pansho go counts as settling his fate only if the character chose it knowingly, after seeing him, not by doing nothing.
- A Pansho wounded by someone else, e.g. Edric, does not cost the character the reward for no blood shed.

The String of Sausages is a micro scenario for characters at experience level zero. Full success gives 250 points.
