# The Crypt: experience rewards

Experience points for the scenario The Crypt. The GM awards them at the end of the scenario (see *Awarding experience points* in `Rules/Experience.md`). Each reward is awarded at most once.

| Achievement                                                                         | Points |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|--------|
| Tobin saved: returns alive to the village                                           | 125    |
| the rite learned: from Hilde or from the fresco in the chapel                       | 25     |
| the revenant's tracks in the ravine found                                           | 25     |
| the swarm of rats in the ossuary driven off or routed                               | 10     |
| the dead dog defeated, or its bones burned or smashed before it rose                | 25     |
| the trap in the antechamber avoided: the character knows of the plate and avoids it | 25     |
| Corwin reaches the village alive: Hilde or the headman's judgement                  | 50     |
| the Corwin revenant destroyed                                                       | 50     |
| Malric defeated in combat: falls at 90%                                             | 75     |
| Malric destroyed for ever: put to rest by the rite, burned, or his head burned      | 140    |

- A character who entered through the crack from the ravine and skipped the antechamber gets no points for the dog or the trap.
- A trap the character stepped on without knowing about it counts as not avoided, even if the character jumped aside.
- The reward for the Corwin revenant excludes the reward for Corwin reaching the village alive.

The Crypt is a mini scenario for characters at experience level zero. Full success gives 500 points. A character who saves Tobin and destroys the revenant usually gets 350–450 points.
