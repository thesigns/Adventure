# Equipment

## Money

1 gold crown = 10 silver = 100 copper. A labourer earns a silver a day, a skilled craftsman two to five.

## Weapons

Wound range from the lightest to the heaviest a weapon can inflict, typical wound in brackets (see *Wounds in combat* in `Rules/Combat.md`).

| Weapon                                 | Wounds                                     |
|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------|
| fist, kick                             | scratch                                    |
| dagger, club, sling                    | scratch – medium wound (light wound)       |
| sword, axe, mace, spear, bow, crossbow | light wound – serious wound (medium wound) |
| two-handed sword, greataxe, warhammer  | medium wound – mortal blow (serious wound) |

## Armour

- **light:** leather, padded jack.
- **medium:** chain mail, scale, brigandine.
- **heavy:** full plate.
- Shield and helmet as in `Rules/Equipment.md`.

## Light

| Source  | Burns                 | Light                          |
|---------|-----------------------|--------------------------------|

| torch   | 1 hour                | about 10 m                     |
| lantern | 4 hours per oil flask | about 10 m, shielded from wind |

## Starting purse

| Status                         | Money        |
|--------------------------------|--------------|
| peasant, beggar, apprentice    | a few copper |
| soldier, craftsman, adventurer | a few silver |
| merchant, knight, mage         | a few gold   |

Every character has clothes, a knife, a waterskin, flint and steel, a backpack and 2 days of rations, plus the typical gear of their profession.
