# History

Dates are counted from the founding of the Kingdom of Valdren (AF, After Founding); the present is around 1240 AF.

## Timeline

- **The elder days:** forests cover most of Generia. Elves rule the woods, dwarves the mountains; humans live in scattered tribes. The elves read the sky and make the calendar that all Generia uses (see *Calendar* in `Culture.md`).
- **The Empire of Aurelion** (about two thousand years ago to 240 AF): human sorcerer-emperors rule from their capital Aurelion in the north-east and unite most of Generia. They build roads, towers, temples and tombs, spread the elven calendar, clear forests and push the elves west, and grow ever hungrier for power, turning at last to necromancy and pacts with demons.
- **The founding of Valdren** (0 AF): the imperial province between Silvermere and the Vael gains its own king, still sworn to the emperor.
- **The Ashfall** (240 AF): a ritual of the last emperor goes wrong. Fire falls from the sky, the capital burns, and the north-east turns into the Ashlands. The empire breaks apart within a generation. Ever since, necromancy and pacts with demons have been crimes in every land.
- **The dark centuries** (240–700 AF): orcs come to the Ashlands and raid across the Crookback Mountains; the imperial roads crumble. Valdren survives as the strongest of the empire's heirs. The Harrowland clans form to hold the passes, and the dwarves seal their holds against goblins and orcs.
- **The rise of Valdren** (700–1100 AF): the kingdom grows rich and settled. Mereton receives its royal charter, the Arcane College is founded in Kingsbridge, and the halflings of Greenhollow accept the king's rule in return for his protection.
- **The push west** (1100 AF to now): settlers cross the Silverrun into the Fenmarch, clearing forest and draining marsh, and quarrels with the elves of Elenwood over the felling of the trees grow sharper.

## The present

- **Kingdom of Valdren:** King Roderick II of House Valcourt, aged 88, has reigned for 41 years: firm and just, but tired. His heir, Prince Aldric (49), is a soldier who would rather fight on the borders than sit at court; his younger sister, Princess Isolde (42), is clever and ambitious, and the court whispers about the succession.
- **Mereton:** ruled by its council of merchants, led by the Lord Mayor, chosen each year by the council.
- **Elenwood:** Queen Elaria has ruled the wood elves for over four centuries. She keeps her people apart from humans and watches the settlers in the Fenmarch with growing anger.
- **Khaz Dorun:** High Thane Thorgar son of Durek rules the dwarven clans of the Ironpeaks and wars without end against the goblins in the caves.
- **The Harrowlands:** with orc raids growing bolder, the clans have elected a war-chief at the moot of Sunstone: Garrick, called Urzog, a half-orc.
- **The Ashlands:** no ruler; orc warlords fight one another, and rumours say something stirs in the ruins of Aurelion.
