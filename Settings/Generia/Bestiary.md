# Bestiary

- Category, toughness and typical wound against an unarmoured human (see *Przeciwnicy* and *Rany w walce* in `Rules/Combat.md`, *Wytrzymałość* in `Rules/Health.md`). Every extra enemy makes the fight one level harder.
- Humanoids with weapons wound as their weapons do (see `Equipment.md`).
- “Feels no wounds”: wounds do not hinder it, it never flees, surrenders or tires (see *Zmęczenie w walce* in `Rules/Combat.md`).
- Everything else about a monster is what fantasy folklore says about it.
