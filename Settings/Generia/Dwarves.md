# Dwarves

- **Holds:** underground cities. Khaz Dorun, the great hold, houses a dozen or more clans, Durnhold a few, lesser holds one or a few; all are sworn to the High Thane.
- **Clan:** kin by blood and oath, with its own **halls** in the hold (forges, homes, tombs of the ancestors), ruled by its elder. A child belongs to the clan whose halls raised it, usually the father's.
- **High Thane:** first among the elders, rules Khaz Dorun and the dwarves of the Ironpeaks (see *The present* in `History.md`).
- **Bonds:** the clan answers for its members' oaths, debts and grudges, and they pass to children (see *Law* in `Culture.md`).
- **Greeting:** name, parent, clan, hold: "Halvar son of Brokk, of clan Stonehelm, of Khaz Dorun". The first question to a strange dwarf is "which clan?": it settles kinship, debts and grudges.
- **Marks:** beard braids, clasps, runes on bracers and belts. A dwarf knows the hold from afar, the clan up close.
- **Struck from the clan:** the worst punishment. The name is not spoken in the hold, nobody vouches for the outcast, and dwarves shun trade, drink and table with them. At the striking the clan cuts off the outcast's braids; from then on they wear the beard loose, and to braid it in clan fashion would be a lie no dwarf forgives. A loose beard or a name given without a clan tells a dwarf everything. The way back: a great deed or atonement accepted by the elder.
- **Abroad:** traders, couriers, bankers and smiths sent by their clan, still of it; or exiles and fortune-seekers.
