# Geography

The map `Maps/GeneriaMap.svg` is the source of truth; this file describes what is on it.

## Continent

- **Generia:** a small continent in the northern hemisphere, about the size of Australia: roughly 3,500 km west to east and 2,500 km north to south. An irregular shape: a long north-western coast running north-east, a large peninsula in the north-east, a broad western lobe, and seven islands offshore.
- **Climate** as in Europe, by latitude. The southern coast is warm like southern Italy and Spain: hot dry summers, mild wet winters. The centre is temperate like France or Germany. The north beyond the Ironpeaks is cool like the lands around Oslo: long snowy winters, short summers, long summer days and short winter ones. Seasons and daylight follow the same latitudes.
- **Map:** 1 px = 1 km, north up, x grows east and y south. Rivers, lakes, forests, mountain ranges and lands carry their names in `data-name`, rivers their length in km in `data-km`, ranges their ridge line in `data-ridge`. Lands without a drawn border have an invisible outline `region-<name>`. The map shows only what everyone knows, so the player may see it in full.
- **Travel:** on foot about 30 km a day on a road, 20 off-road; on horseback about 50. Roads between towns are patrolled poorly, wilderness not at all.

## Mountains

- **Ironpeaks:** the great range of young, high and jagged mountains. It runs from the west across the middle of the continent and bends north-east, with many spurs on both sides. Home of the dwarven clans; the great hold is Khaz Dorun.
- **Crookback Mountains:** the second range, in the east, running from north-west to south-east down to the eastern coast. Far older than the Ironpeaks, lower and worn into bare ridges. A valley separates it from the Ironpeaks. Its few passes lead into the Ashlands.

## Waters

- **Silvermere:** the largest lake, at the southern foot of the Ironpeaks, so wide that on a grey day the far shore cannot be seen. Fed by the Highrun and the Aldwater, drained by the Silverrun to the southern sea. Storms come quickly, and the deep water is said to hide things older than Aurelion.
- **Mistmere and Reedmere:** two smaller lakes west of Silvermere. The Mistbrook and the Reedbrook flow into them from the mountains; the Brenna drains Mistmere and the Wend drains Reedmere to the southern sea.
- **Vael:** the longest river, from the Ironpeaks south-east to the eastern coast. It is the border between Valdren and the Harrowlands.
- **Aelwen:** from the Ironpeaks through Elenwood to the south-western coast.
- **Coldwater and Frostrun:** short rivers flowing north from the Ironpeaks through the Frostmarch to the northern coast.

## Lands

- **Kingdom of Valdren:** the human heartland, between Silvermere, the Silverrun and the Aldwater in the west and the Vael in the east, from the Ironpeaks down to the southern coast. King, nobles, knights, peasants. Capital: Kingsbridge. Forests: Blackwood, Fernwood, Hartwood and smaller woods.
- **Greenhollow:** halfling land in the south of Valdren, under the king. Low, grassy glacial hills, fertile and tilled: fields, orchards, vineyards and small woods between them.
- **The Fenmarch:** the frontier west of Valdren, the river lands of Mistmere and Reedmere and the rivers flowing into and out of the lakes, from the Ironpeaks to the southern coast. Forests (Silverwood, Southwood), marshes and rivers. Independent villages and small towns, not part of Valdren, where humanity pushes west. The marshes hide will-o'-wisps, bog hags, lizardfolk and the drowned dead.
- **Elenwood:** the great primeval forest in the west, the largest remnant of the forests that once covered Generia, the realm of the wood elves. Outsiders enter only with permission. Highwood, under the Ironpeaks north of Silvermere, is a smaller forest east of it.
- **The Harrowlands:** between the Vael and the Crookback Mountains. Not a kingdom but a loose bond of clans, each led by a chieftain; in war the clans elect a war-chief at a moot. Herders, hunters and sellswords, hard and plain people used to hardship, among them many half-orcs, who are equals here as nowhere else. They guard the passes against the orcs of the Ashlands. Valdren sees them as savages but gladly hires their warriors.
- **Ashlands:** blasted wastes beyond the Crookback Mountains, up to the north-eastern coast and its peninsula, roamed by orc tribes. Orc raids come through the passes.
- **Frostmarch:** the cold north beyond the Ironpeaks, between the mountains and the northern coast, with coniferous forests (Coldpines, Frostwood). Few people and scattered settlements of humans and elves, dwarven holds on the northern slopes of the Ironpeaks, no halflings. A refuge for outlaws and exiles from every land.
