# Geography

## Continent

- **Generia:** a small continent of kingdoms, forests, mountains and old ruins in the northern hemisphere, about the size of Australia: roughly 3,500 km west to east and 2,500 km north to south.
- **Climate** as in Europe, by latitude. The south is warm like southern Italy and Spain: hot dry summers, mild wet winters. The centre is temperate like France or Germany. The north is cool like the lands around Oslo: long snowy winters, short summers, long summer days and short winter ones. Seasons and daylight follow the same latitudes.
- **Travel:** on foot about 30 km a day on a road, 20 off-road; on horseback about 50. Roads between towns are patrolled poorly, wilderness not at all.

## Lands

- **Kingdom of Valdren:** the human heartland in the centre. King, nobles, knights, peasants. Capital: Kingsbridge, on the river Vael.
- **Free cities of the coast:** merchant republics ruled by guild councils. The largest is Port Seravel, home of thieves' guilds, wizards and every kind of trade.
- **Elenwood:** an ancient forest in the west, the largest remnant of the forests that once covered Generia, the realm of the wood elves. Outsiders enter only with permission.
- **Ironpeaks:** mountains in the north, home of the dwarven clans. The great hold is Khaz Dorun.
- **Greenhollow:** peaceful halfling farmland south of Valdren: low, grassy glacial hills with small woods and fields between them.
- **Ashlands:** blasted wastes in the east, roamed by orc tribes.
- **Mirefen:** vast marshes in the south-east, between Greenhollow and the Ashlands, where the river Vael spreads into a delta before the sea. Reed villages on stilts, fishers and eel catchers, smugglers' channels, fever in summer. Will-o'-wisps, bog hags, lizardfolk and the drowned dead.
- **Silvermere:** a great lake in the north-west, between Elenwood and the Ironpeaks, so wide that on a grey day the far shore cannot be seen. Lake towns, fishing fleets, ferries and islands with old ruins. Storms come quickly, the lake freezes at the edges in winter, and the deep water is said to hide things older than Aurelion.
- **Frostmarch:** the northernmost lands beyond the mountains, with the harshest winters: barbarian tribes, giants and frost.
