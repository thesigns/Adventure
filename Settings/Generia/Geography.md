# Geography

The map `Maps/GeneriaMap.svg` is the source of truth; this file describes what is on it.

## Continent

- **Generia:** a small continent in the northern hemisphere, about the size of Australia: roughly 3,500 km west to east and 2,500 km north to south. An irregular shape: a long north-western coast running north-east, a large peninsula in the north-east, a broad western lobe, and seven islands offshore.
- **Climate** as in Europe, by latitude. The southern coast is warm like southern Italy and Spain: hot dry summers, mild wet winters. The centre is temperate like France or Germany. The north beyond the Ironpeaks is cool like the lands around Oslo: long snowy winters, short summers, long summer days and short winter ones. Seasons and daylight follow the same latitudes.
- **Map:** 1 px = 1 km, north up, x grows east and y south. Rivers, lakes, forests, mountain ranges and lands carry their names in `data-name`, rivers their length in km in `data-km`, ranges their ridge line in `data-ridge`. Lands without a drawn border have an invisible outline `region-<name>`. The map shows only what everyone knows, so the player may see it in full.
- **Travel:** on foot about 30 km a day on a road, 20 off-road; on horseback about 50. Roads between towns are patrolled poorly, wilderness not at all.

## Mountains

- **Ironpeaks:** the great range of young, high and jagged mountains. It runs from the west across the middle of the continent and bends north-east, with many spurs on both sides. Home of the dwarven clans. Their great hold is Khaz Dorun, high in the middle of the range where it bends north-east; Durnhold is a smaller hold on the northern slopes, above the Frostmarch. The gnomes' home is Brasshollow, a warren of workshops in the western Ironpeaks, north of Elenwood.
- **Crookback Mountains:** the second range, in the east, running from north-west to south-east down to the eastern coast. Far older than the Ironpeaks, lower and worn into bare ridges. A valley separates it from the Ironpeaks; it is the main way between the Harrowlands and the Ashlands, guarded by the clan fort of Ashgate. Over the range itself leads Crow's Pass, the only pass worth the name.

## Waters

- **Silvermere:** the largest lake, at the southern foot of the Ironpeaks, so wide that on a grey day the far shore cannot be seen. Fed by the Highrun and the Aldwater, drained by the Silverrun to the southern sea. Storms come quickly, and the deep water is said to hide things older than Aurelion.
- **Mistmere and Reedmere:** two smaller lakes west of Silvermere. The Mistbrook and the Reedbrook flow into them from the mountains; the Brenna drains Mistmere and the Wend drains Reedmere to the southern sea.
- **Vael:** the longest river, from the Ironpeaks south-east to the eastern coast. It is the border between Valdren and the Harrowlands.
- **Kingswater:** a tributary of the Vael, from the foot of the Ironpeaks through Kingsbridge.
- **Aelwen:** from the Ironpeaks through Elenwood to the south-western coast.
- **Coldwater and Frostrun:** short rivers flowing north from the Ironpeaks through the Frostmarch to the northern coast.

## Ruins of Aurelion

The Empire of Aurelion fell a thousand years ago. Its ruins are scattered across Generia, full of treasure and danger; the best known are on the map:

- **Aurelion:** the ruined imperial capital, deep in the Ashlands. Orcs camp in its shadow but avoid its halls.
- **Drowned Spires:** towers sinking into the Fenmarch marshes, between Mistmere, Reedmere and the sea.
- **Hollow Crown:** a ring of broken halls on a hill in the western Frostmarch.
- **Lonely Tower:** a tower on an island off the north-western coast.
- **Sunstone:** a fallen temple of the sun in the Harrowlands, where the clans hold their moots.

## Lands

- **Kingdom of Valdren:** the human heartland, between Silvermere, the Silverrun and the Aldwater in the west and the Vael in the east, from the Ironpeaks down to the southern coast. King, nobles, knights, peasants. Capital: Kingsbridge, in the north of the kingdom on the Kingswater, just south of Fernwood, near the Vael and the mountains. Second city: Mereton, on the north-eastern shore of Silvermere at the mouth of the Aldwater, the last bastion of civilisation before the Fenmarch. Goods from the mountains come down the Aldwater and spread by boat across the lake and into the Fenmarch. A merchant city with a royal charter, governed by a council of merchants, where traders, adventurers and Fenmarch folk mix with royal officials. Forests: Blackwood, Fernwood, Hartwood and smaller woods.
- **Greenhollow:** halfling land in the south of Valdren, under the king. Low, grassy glacial hills, fertile and tilled: fields, orchards, vineyards and small woods between them. The main town is Hollowbury, at the end of the road from Kingsbridge, west of Hazelwood.
- **The Fenmarch:** the frontier west of Valdren, the river lands of Mistmere and Reedmere and the rivers flowing into and out of the lakes, from the Ironpeaks to the southern coast. Forests (Silverwood, Southwood), marshes and rivers. Independent villages and small towns, not part of Valdren, where humanity pushes west. The marshes hide will-o'-wisps, bog hags, lizardfolk and the drowned dead.
- **Elenwood:** the great primeval forest in the west, the largest remnant of the forests that once covered Generia, the realm of the wood elves. Outsiders enter only with permission. The elves' chief seat is Aelindor, on the eastern bank of the Aelwen in the heart of the forest. Highwood, under the Ironpeaks north of Silvermere, is a smaller forest east of it.
- **The Harrowlands:** between the Vael and the Crookback Mountains. Not a kingdom but a loose bond of clans, each led by a chieftain; in war the clans elect a war-chief at a moot. Herders, hunters and sellswords, hard and plain people used to hardship, among them many half-orcs, who are equals here as nowhere else. They guard the way through Ashgate and Crow's Pass against the orcs of the Ashlands. Valdren sees them as savages but gladly hires their warriors.
- **Ashlands:** blasted wastes beyond the Crookback Mountains, up to the north-eastern coast and its peninsula, roamed by orc tribes. Orc raids come through the valley by Ashgate and over Crow's Pass.
- **Frostmarch:** the cold north beyond the Ironpeaks, between the mountains and the northern coast, with coniferous forests (Coldpines, Frostwood). Few people and scattered settlements of humans and elves, dwarven holds on the northern slopes of the Ironpeaks, no halflings. A refuge for outlaws and exiles from every land.
