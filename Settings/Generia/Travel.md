# Travel

Distances come from the map: the `<desc>` at the top of `Maps/ElandriaMap.svg` lists the roads and rivers with their lengths and every place with its coordinates (1 px = 1 km).

## Pace

| Way of travel                  | km a day | Notes                                              |
|--------------------------------|----------|----------------------------------------------------|
| on foot, road                  | 30       |                                                    |
| on foot, open country          | 20       | fields, moors, light woods                         |
| on foot, forest, hills         | 15       |                                                    |
| on foot, mountains, marsh      | 10       | passes may be closed in winter                     |
| on horseback, road             | 50       | the horse needs rest one day in seven              |
| on horseback, open country     | 30       | no faster than on foot in forest, mountains, marsh |
| wagon or caravan               | 25       | roads only                                         |
| riverboat                      | 60 / 20  | downstream / upstream                              |
| sailing ship along the coast   | 120      | less in bad weather, nothing in a storm            |

- **Off the road** the way is about 1.3 times the straight-line distance on the map, more in mountains, forests and marshes.
- **Forced march:** half as far again in a day, but the character must roll (average) or arrive exhausted: all actions one level harder until a night's rest.
- **Example:** Kingsbridge to Mereton is 506 km by road: about 17 days on foot, 10 on horseback.

## Costs

| Item                                   | Price        |
|----------------------------------------|--------------|
| place with a merchant caravan          | 2 s a day    |
| passage on a riverboat                 | 2 s a day    |
| passage on a ship, with food           | 5 s a day    |
| ferry across a river or Silvermere     | 5 c to 1 s   |
| horse hire                             | 1 s a day    |
| toll at a bridge, gate or clan border  | 1 c to 1 s   |

## On the road

- **Food and water:** one ration a day (see *Consumables* in `Rules/Equipment.md`). Off the road a character can forage instead of eating rations: a roll a day, easy in summer woods, hard in winter or in the Ashlands.
- **Rest:** a night in an inn or a camp with a watch is proper rest; a night alone in the wild without shelter is not (see *Refreshing* in `Rules/Determination.md`).
- **Getting lost:** off the road without a guide or a map, the character rolls each day (average, easy for a ranger or hunter); on a failure they lose the day.
- **Seasons:** autumn rains turn roads to mud (pace on foot and by wagon drops by a third), winter closes the mountain passes and the Frostmarch, spring floods the Fenmarch.

## Trouble on the way

Once a day of travel the GM rolls whether anything happens, with a table set by the land:

| Land                                   | Trouble on    | Typical trouble                                           |
|----------------------------------------|---------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|
| Valdren and Greenhollow, on roads      | 3–4           | bandits, a toll collector, a patrol asking questions      |
| Valdren off the roads, the Fenmarch    | 3–5           | outlaws, wolves, marsh fever, bog creatures               |
| Elenwood                               | 3–6           | elven wardens turning trespassers back, forest beasts     |
| the Harrowlands                        | 3–6           | clan warriors demanding a toll, raiders from the Ashlands |
| the Ironpeaks                          | 3–7           | goblins, rockfalls, avalanches, cold                      |
| the Frostmarch                         | 3–7           | cold, wolves, outlaws and exiles                          |
| the Ashlands                           | 3–9           | orc warbands, thirst, ash storms                          |
| at sea or on Silvermere                | 3–5           | storms, pirates, sea or lake monsters                     |

On the lowest results the trouble is serious (an ambush, a monster), on the rest minor or only a sign of danger (tracks, smoke, a burned farm). The GM draws the encounter from the land's description in `Geography.md` and from `Bestiary.md`. On a quiet road the GM sums up the days in a sentence.
